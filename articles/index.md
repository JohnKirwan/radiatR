# Articles

### All vignettes

- [Guide for AI
  agents](https://johnkirwan.github.io/radiatR/articles/agent-guide.md):

  Conventions, failure behaviour and pitfalls an AI agent (or anyone
  scripting radiatR) needs before analysing directional data.

- [Circular Statistics and Distribution
  Overlays](https://johnkirwan.github.io/radiatR/articles/circular-statistics.md):

  Dispersion summaries, parametric fits, hypothesis tests with
  multiple-comparison correction, circular correlation, and rose,
  density and KDE distribution overlays for a headings data frame.

- [radiatR design
  contract](https://johnkirwan.github.io/radiatR/articles/design.md):

  The design rules behind the radiatR API: which objects are rich
  classes, which stay plain tibbles, and the conventions the functions
  share.

- [Working with
  Loaders](https://johnkirwan.github.io/radiatR/articles/loaders.md):

  Turning tracking exports into Tracks: experiment manifests,
  read_tracks() and read_tracks_dir(), and registering a custom loader
  dialect or format.

- [radiatR](https://johnkirwan.github.io/radiatR/articles/radiatR.md):

  The main workflow: from tracking files or a table of angles, through
  heading derivation and circular summaries, to a publication-ready
  radiate() plot.

- [Simulating
  Trajectories](https://johnkirwan.github.io/radiatR/articles/simulate.md):

  Generating synthetic trajectories on the unit disk with
  simulate_tracks(), for pipeline testing, teaching, and power analysis.

- [Quantifying path
  shape](https://johnkirwan.github.io/radiatR/articles/track-metrics.md):

  Per-trajectory spatial metrics – distance travelled, straightness, and
  displacement- versus turning-based tortuosity – on recorded x/y
  coordinates.
